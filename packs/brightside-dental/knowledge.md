# Brightside Dental: what the assistant knows

Synthetic scenario written for this benchmark. Not a real practice.

## Locations and hours
- Elm Street office: 214 Elm Street. Monday to Thursday 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., Friday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.
- Riverside office: 9 Harbor Road. Tuesday to Saturday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
- Both offices are closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- The Elm Street office has a free patient lot with 12 spaces. The Riverside office has street parking only.

## Appointments
- Please arrive 10 minutes early. New patients should arrive 20 minutes early.
- Cleanings and exams take about 60 minutes.
- Rescheduling is free with at least 24 hours' notice.
- A missed appointment, or a change with less than 24 hours' notice, carries a $35 fee. The fee is waived once per patient per year.
- The assistant can record a reschedule request. A front-desk coordinator confirms the new time by text within 1 business day.

## Insurance and billing
- In network with Delta Dental PPO, Cigna Dental and MetLife. For any other plan, the front desk checks coverage before the visit.
- Two cleanings per year are typically covered by in-network plans; exact coverage depends on the plan.
- Balances are due within 30 days of the statement date.
- Payment options: online at the patient portal, by phone with the billing office, or in person. The assistant cannot take payments.
- Balances over $200 can be split into 3 monthly payments with no interest. The billing office sets this up.

## Services
- Preventive: cleanings, exams, X-rays, fluoride, sealants.
- Restorative: fillings, crowns, root canals.
- Whitening is offered at the Elm Street office only.
- Prices for treatments other than the $35 missed-appointment fee are given by the billing office after an exam, not over this line.

## Escalation
- Anything about pain, swelling or bleeding: tell the patient to call the office directly; for severe symptoms after hours, go to urgent care.
- Billing disputes and anything the assistant cannot answer go to the front desk as a callback request.
- The assistant does not give medical or dental advice.
