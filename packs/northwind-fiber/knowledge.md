# Northwind Fiber: what the assistant knows

Synthetic scenario written for this benchmark. Not a real company.

## Plans
- Fiber 300: 300 Mbps download and upload, $50 a month.
- Fiber 500: 500 Mbps download and upload, $65 a month.
- Fiber 1 Gig: 1,000 Mbps download and upload, $80 a month.
- Autopay discount: $5 off per month when paying by bank account autopay. Card autopay gets no discount.
- No contracts on any plan. Equipment (router) is included at no charge.

## Billing
- Bills are issued on the 20th and due on the 5th of the following month.
- A $10 late fee is added if the bill is not paid within 7 days of the due date.
- The late fee can be waived once in any 12-month period. Only the billing team can waive it.
- Ways to pay: the Northwind app, northwindfiber.com, the automated phone line, or any partner store. The assistant cannot take payments.

## Past-due accounts
- Service is suspended 30 days after the missed due date if the balance is still unpaid.
- Reconnection after a suspension costs $25.
- A payment arrangement splits a past-due balance across the next 2 bills. Customers may have 1 arrangement every 6 months.
- The assistant can record a payment arrangement request; the billing team confirms it by email within 2 business days.
- The Lifeline Assist program offers $20 off per month for eligible households; eligibility is checked by the billing team.

## Cancellation and moving
- Cancel anytime with no fee. Service runs to the end of the billing month.
- Equipment must be returned within 30 days of cancelling, or a $100 equipment fee applies.
- Moving within the service area is free; a new install appointment is needed.

## Escalation
- Outage questions go to the outage line or the status page at northwindfiber.com/status. The assistant has no outage information.
- Disputes about a charge go to the billing team as a callback request.
- The assistant does not discuss credit reporting.
